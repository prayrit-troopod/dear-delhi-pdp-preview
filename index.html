     The air around you is more than something you breathe. 

    Most people think about the air they breathe only when it becomes difficult to breathe. But your respiratory system is working every minute of every day. Here’s what you need to know about your airways, mucus clearance, oxidative stress — and how Dear Delhi is designed to support your body’s natural defences.

    Why Should You Care About the Effects of Pollution?

    In a city like Delhi, your lungs don't get to choose the air.

    Dust. Smoke. Traffic. Particulate matter. Irritants.

    You breathe them in every day. And some environmental pollutants can contribute to the formation of free radicals — highly reactive molecules that can cause oxidative stress when they overwhelm the body's natural defences.

    Your body already has its own antioxidant systems to deal with them. But when environmental exposure is constant, supporting those natural defences becomes important.

    At the same time, your airways are constantly working to trap and clear particles through mucus and other natural defence mechanisms.

    You can't always change the air around you.
    But you can support your body's natural defences.

    A Formula Built Around Your Body's Natural Defences

    Your respiratory system doesn't rely on one thing to keep doing its job.

    It needs to clear what shouldn't stay in your airways, manage oxidative stress, and maintain healthy respiratory function.

    That's why Dear Delhi brings together 600 mg NAC with a targeted blend of botanical ingredients — including lemongrass, turmeric, eucalyptus, ginger, purple corn, menthol and Puricyn.

    Supports normal mucus clearance and airway comfort.

    Provides targeted botanical support for healthy respiratory function.

    For the avidly curious

    What is GATACA Dear Delhi?

    Dear Delhi is a daily respiratory-support formula combining 600 mg NAC with a targeted botanical blend. It is designed to support normal mucus clearance, antioxidant defence and healthy respiratory function.

    What is NAC and why is it included?

    NAC (N-Acetyl-L-Cysteine) is a source of cysteine, which the body uses to produce glutathione, an important antioxidant. NAC plays a role in supporting normal mucus clearance.

    Does Dear Delhi detox or clean the lungs?

    Dear Delhi is not a treatment product. It is formulated to provide nutritional support for the body's natural respiratory and antioxidant defence systems.

    Can I take Dear Delhi every day?

    Yes. Dear Delhi is designed for daily use at the recommended serving of one sachet per day. Do not exceed the recommended daily serving.

    How do I take Dear Delhi?

    Simply tear, squeeze and take one sachet directly into your mouth. No water or mixing is required.

    Is Dear Delhi useful for people living in polluted cities?

    Dear Delhi is designed for people looking for daily respiratory and antioxidant support, particularly those who experience everyday environmental exposure such as dust, smoke and urban air pollution.

    Can smokers take Dear Delhi?

    Dear Delhi is not intended to treat or reverse the effects of smoking. Smokers or people exposed to second-hand smoke should speak with a healthcare professional if they have respiratory symptoms or concerns.

    Can I take Dear Delhi if I have asthma, COPD or another respiratory condition?

    If you have asthma, COPD or another medical condition, consult your healthcare professional before using it.

    Does Dear Delhi contain only NAC?

    No. Alongside 600 mg NAC, Dear Delhi contains a targeted blend of lemongrass, turmeric, eucalyptus oil, Puricyn, ginger, purple corn and menthol.

    Why is Dear Delhi a liquid sachet?

    The ready-to-use liquid format makes daily supplementation simple and portable. No water, mixing or preparation is required.

    Who should not take Dear Delhi?

    Dear Delhi is intended for adults. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition, or take prescription medication, consult your healthcare professional before use.

    How should I store Dear Delhi?

    Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep the sachets sealed until use.
